# Broadcast News Package — our demo

One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

Demo: *The Week in AI* (本周 AI 三件事, 54.0 s) · `news-package.mp4` · subtitles `news-package.srt` (external only) · source in [`demo/`](demo/)

LEMO AI NEWS, LemoLab's own channel, reports three stories from the week of 12–18 October 2026: Google opens Gemini 4 Argon to every developer at an introductory price that doubles on 1 December; Korea's Kospi falls 7.57 % in a day, the seventh losing session in a row, and ends more than 20 % below its September high; the EU's AI Office sends questions to OpenAI and Anthropic after two incidents with AI agents (as reported by Euronews), and in Dublin the Commission's Executive Vice-President says the office "has started enforcing these rules". Every number and sentence is checked against the sources in [`demo/FACTS.md`](demo/FACTS.md); the Kospi line is drawn from real daily closes.

This is an unofficial film made from public information (company pages, exchange data, news reports, a European Commission speech). Google, OpenAI, Anthropic, Samsung, SK hynix, Euronews and the European Commission did not make or endorse it; their names are set in type, never as logos. Two background plates (a server aisle, a silicon wafer) are AI-generated illustrations and carry an "ILLUSTRATION / 示意" tag whenever they are on screen. There is no anchor on camera: the studio is an empty virtual set drawn in code.

## Story & structure

Shape: a bulletin round-up, bookended by the studio. Sting (0–1.5 s) → the virtual studio lights up and the camera pushes into the video wall (1.5–3.0) → the rundown of three stories (3.0–6.5) → bumper 01 → story ① (7.5–15.0) → bumper 02 → story ② (16.0–28.5) → bumper 03 → story ③ (29.5–43.7) → the rundown returns and is ticked off, the camera pulls back out of the wall into the studio (44.0–47.7) → end card (48.0–54.0).

Each story follows the package's order of a fact. ①: the name (big type) → the price change (split-flap). ②: where (locator map) → the line breaking the threshold, with the one-day drop, close, streak and distance from the September high on a readout → who it hit (two chip stocks). ③: who sent what (a map: two requests fly out of Brussels to the two labs, each with the incident behind it) → what the official said (a quote card from the Commission's own speech) → the verdict stamp.

Formats spent, each for a new fact: sting that becomes the bug; virtual studio with a push through the video wall and the reverse pull; rundown (headline menu) opened and ticked off; three numbered bumpers; one lower-third strap held from the first story to the recap, flipping eight times, including a profile strap; source line per story; illustration corner tag; big-type title with a GA stamp and a typed model ID; split-flap price board; locator map; line chart of real closes breaking a −20 % threshold after one bar of silence, with streak counter and readout panel; two falling bars with a reference line and badge; explainer map long take (members lighting from the city, push, pan); documents flying out of a city, highlighter on the reason; a season badge; quote card with underline and stamp; band wipe, hard cuts on timpani, slat bumpers, slanted wipe; end card.

`readcheck.mjs` passes on every key text (strap headlines, price board, chart readout, bars, map labels, documents, quote, end card) except three deliberate previews: rows 01 and 03 of the opening rundown (they return for 2 s+ in the recap and as straps) and the Seoul label on the 1.5 s locator (spoken in the voice). The sting's wordmark, the GA stamp, the typed model ID, the KOSPI title on the locator and the ENFORCING stamp are left out of `TEXTS` as ornaments: each restates a strap or a line that is read elsewhere.

## Score structure

- 120 BPM, E minor news theme: spiccato violins (root–fifth–octave eighths), pizzicato bass on the beat, horn pads, a low-passed 16th-note saw pulse, kick on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4.
- Sting: tremolo violins → brass/timpani/crash tutti on the plate slam → glockenspiel run on the glint. Studio: light clunks, a timpani on the lights, a long whoosh and glockenspiel on the push.
- Bumpers (6.5, 15.0, 28.5): the band drops for half a beat; timpani + bass drum, a brass stab, two glockenspiel notes a tone higher for each item.
- Story ①: full theme. Story ②: violins an octave down, tremolo pads, four-on-the-floor kick, trading-room ticks; the music stops for one beat at 20.5 and the break at 21.0 is the heaviest hit of the film (low brass, timpani E2, crash, bass drum), then a timpani roll and a tubular bell on the stamp. Story ③: pizzicato and horns only, soft air under the map, a highlighter squeak per reason. After the ENFORCING stamp (42.1) only cellos, contrabass and a horn hold a low E until the recap.
- Recap: theme returns, a glockenspiel note per tick. End card: timpani roll into an E-major tutti (trumpets, horns, trombones, violins, cellos) with a tubular bell and a glockenspiel arpeggio.
- Voice: Kokoro `af_heart`, eight lines, speed 1.0–1.12; "Lemo" written `Lemmo`, "Gemini 4" `Gemini Four`. Music ducked ≈ 7 dB and thinned in 1.2–5 kHz under speech. Master −14.1 LUFS, peak −1.1 dBFS. Speech-to-text of the final mix matches the script.

## End card

From 48.0 s, held six seconds: dateline "LEMO AI NEWS · WEEK 42 · 12–18 OCT 2026", "本周 AI 三件事" (120 px), "The Week in AI" (lemon italic), a thin rule, "LEMO AI NEWS · 电视新闻包装 BROADCAST NEWS PACKAGE", "LemoLab × Claude Opus 5.5", the large plate and wordmark on the right, then five lines: news sources, data and speech sources, CC0 samples and public-domain map data, OFL fonts, the Kokoro voice and the two AI-generated plates. There is no CC BY material in this film. The "LemoLab × Claude Opus 5.5" sign-off belongs to this library's demo only; a user's film carries no LemoLab credit and no copy of this card. No mascot, no watermark.

## Build notes

```

  index.html, main.js     the page: package parts (strap, bug, tags, source line), studio(), bumper(), shotLocator(), shotKospi(), shotEU()/doc(), shotQuote(); SCENES table; EV for the mix; CUES for the .srt
  mix.py                  score (sampled), foley from events.json, voice, ducking
  vo/lines.json           narration (Kokoro)
  data/                   KOSPI daily closes (CSV + raw API responses)
  gen/                    the two illustrative plates (+ one unused)
  tools/prep.py           plate grade (duotone) + Natural Earth → Europe and East Asia projections
  tools/fonts.sh          OFL subsets for every character in main.js
  tools/subs.py           CUES → ../news-package.srt
  tools/gen_plates.sh, gen_image.py   regenerate plates (Codex image_gen)
  tools/footage.py        footage finder with licence logging (not needed here; see STYLE.md §10)
  FACTS.md, SOURCES.md, CREDITS, TREATMENT.md, PRODUCTION_LOG.md
```

`sh demo/build.sh` (library set up with `setup.sh deps voice music`, sample packs vsco2ce / vcsl): fonts → plates and maps → voice + speech check → reading check → events → render (1,620 frames, 30 fps) → mix → master (−14 LUFS, no grain) → .srt → poster and styleframe (22.6 s, the broken threshold).

Demo-specific choices and pitfalls: the first cut of this film said the EU had used the AI Act "for the first time" and that there were "no fines"; the Commission's own speeches showed the first requests for information went out in August and neither source mentions fines, so story ③ was rebuilt around what the sources do say. "Bear market" depends on the peak: from June's record close the Kospi had been more than 20 % down since July; the film uses the September high, as the report does, and says so on the axis. A one-line "worst week since 2008" was dropped because the primary reports say it of two stocks, not of the index. Naver's single-stock figures disagree with the reported ones, so only the index comes from Naver.
