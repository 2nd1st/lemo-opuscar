## F5 惠特曼引文

- Walt Whitman, Leaves of Grass (Boston: Thayer and Eldridge, 1860), p.380, "Crossing Brooklyn Ferry" 第 6 节。扫描：University of California Libraries / Internet Archive — https://archive.org/details/whitleavesofgrass00whitrich/page/380
  原文（已对照扫描页逐字核对）："Just as any of you is one of a living crowd, I was one of a crowd,"
  书页镜头里整句可见；字卡只取后半句 "I was one of a crowd,"（前加省略号、全大写、保留逗号），中文"我也曾在人群里。"为本片自译（"也"承接前半句 "Just as any of you…"）。
